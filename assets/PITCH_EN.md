# WHITE-ROSE.AI

### The operating system for destination weddings in Italy

---

## 1. Executive Summary

White-Rose.ai is an AI-powered collaborative platform that takes destination weddings from chaos to a bookable plan.

The product is not another supplier marketplace.

It is a shared operating workspace where international couples, wedding planners, venues, hotels and suppliers work on the same project, with AI reducing friction, fragmentation and administrative work.

The experience starts with an AI Wedding Concierge that talks with the couple and turns preferences, budget, destination, style, guests and logistical constraints into a structured Wedding Brief.

From that brief, White-Rose.ai activates the best-fit partners, organizes proposals and quotes, keeps conversations and documents in the same workspace and guides the project toward booking, budget and coordination.

The goal is not to replace wedding planners or suppliers.

The goal is to amplify them.

White-Rose.ai aims to become the digital infrastructure for the Italian destination wedding ecosystem and, over time, the European Wedding Operating System for premium multi-supplier events.

---

# 2. The Problem

Italy sells the dream.

The process to deliver it is still broken.

An international couple needs to coordinate:

* wedding planners
* venues
* hotels
* catering
* photographers and videographers
* floral designers
* musicians
* transportation
* guest experiences
* legal documentation
* local logistics

The process still happens across email, WhatsApp, PDFs, spreadsheets, phone calls and quotes in different formats.

The result:

* no single source of truth
* duplicated information
* quotes that are difficult to compare
* fragmented communication
* slow response cycles
* repetitive administrative work for professionals

Marketplaces generate lists.

White-Rose.ai orchestrates the project.

The wedding industry has adopted digital marketing.

It has not yet adopted digital operations.

---

# 3. Solution

White-Rose.ai turns a chat into an operating wedding project.

Example:

"We would like to get married on Lake Como or in Tuscany, 120 international guests, natural elegant style, EUR 70k budget, with hotels and transfers for guests."

The platform produces:

* structured Wedding Brief
* shortlist of qualified partners
* comparable proposals and quotes
* shared budget and timeline
* chat and documents connected to the project
* multilingual support
* automatic summaries and tasks
* coordination between the couple and professionals
* path toward booking and commerce orchestration

AI is not the final product.

It is the layer that turns qualified interest into booked weddings.

# 5. Why Now

Three trends are converging.

## AI has become practical

Conversational AI can understand complex requests, maintain context and support long decision-making processes.

Wedding planning is ideal for this kind of support.

## Weddings are still offline

The market generates billions, but operations remain manual.

There is no shared platform connecting couples, planners, venues and suppliers throughout the full wedding lifecycle.

## Italy is an ideal market

Italy is one of the most desirable wedding destinations in the world.

Destination weddings generate higher budgets, more hospitality services and greater logistical complexity.

That complexity increases the value of an intelligent operating platform.

# 7. Business Model

The initial model is deliberately simple.

The platform will be free for couples and partners in the early phase, reducing adoption barriers and accelerating network growth.

Revenue will be generated through a commission on weddings successfully closed and booked through White-Rose.ai.

Logic:

* no upfront cost for the couple
* no initial subscription for partners
* revenue aligned with project success
* growing GMV as more weddings are managed

Over time, the platform can add:

* premium partner services
* payment layer
* booking orchestration
* travel and hospitality services
* advanced operating tools

# 8. Go To Market

White-Rose.ai starts from Italy for two strategic reasons.

First, the country is one of the most prestigious destination wedding markets in the world.

Second, the founding team already has established relationships with wedding planners, venues, hospitality operators and local suppliers.

This reduces supply acquisition risk and enables a launch with qualified partners.
